Mallampati & Cormack–Lehane Grader

Document observed airway anatomy using two distinct ordinal classification systems. This tool provides descriptions, not a combined score or a prediction of difficult intubation.

Clinical limitation: Modified Mallampati is a preoperative oropharyngeal examination; Cormack–Lehane describes the view during direct laryngoscopy. One does not determine the other. Use clinical assessment and locally approved airway protocols for management.
